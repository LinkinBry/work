Chapter 2: Charlotte the Spider

Life in the barn was busy, but Wilbur still felt lonely. The geese honked, the sheep muttered, and Templeton the rat scurried about, but none of them wanted to play.

One evening, as Wilbur lay in his pen feeling sad, he heard a kind voice in the darkness. "I’ll be your friend," the voice said. Wilbur looked up and saw a gray spider hanging from her web. Her name was Charlotte. She explained, “I spin webs and catch flies. It may not sound beautiful, but it keeps me alive.” Wilbur’s heart leapt with joy. “You’re wonderful! I’ve never had a friend before.” From that moment, Charlotte promised to stand by him, and Wilbur finally felt happy knowing he wasn’t alone anymore.
